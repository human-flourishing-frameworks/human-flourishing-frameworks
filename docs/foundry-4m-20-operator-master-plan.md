# Foundry 4M / 20-Operator Master Plan

Status: execution plan, not proof of revenue.

This plan is plain operational scaffolding for starting with 20 people. It is
not current traction, not committed revenue, not investor evidence, and not a
guarantee.
It also does not claim that 20 operators are already active, that 4M revenue is
already won, or that foundry pooling is implemented.

Allowed claim: the repo contains a consent-first plan to recruit 20 operators,
validate useful paid work, and later add resource sharing only after explicit
per-resource opt-in.

## Target Math

If 4M means annual gross revenue:

```text
$4,000,000 / 12 = $333,334 monthly gross target
$4,000,000 / 20 operators = $200,000 annual gross contribution per operator
$333,334 / 20 = $16,667 monthly gross contribution per operator
```

That is a high target. It requires high-ticket services, managed delivery pods,
enterprise pilots, founder-led closing, or a mix of all four. The first goal is
not to prove the full target. The first goal is to discover which offer gets a
real buyer response.

come first. Resource sharing comes later and starts off.

## Resource Pool Gate

Resource sharing is not part of the first pitch. It may only begin after all of
these are true:

- ~/.foundry/consent.json defaults every resource off.
- Per-resource opt-in only.
- 60-second withdrawal.
- Visible purpose field.
- Hard caps on every resource.
- No personal files/browser/email/webcam/PII.
- No remote override.
- No training on private operator data without explicit, separate consent.

## First 24 Hours

1. Freeze the claim: 20-operator trust-first AI foundry, consent-first,
   local-first, no hidden pooling.
2. Pick one paid offer.
3. Pick one buyer segment.
4. Assign the provisional 20-operator role map.
5. Create one demo packet and one private delivery checklist.
6. Book 10 conversations.
7. Track every contact: person, problem, offer, next step, consent notes.

## First 7 Days

- 50 outreach messages.
- 20 discovery calls requested.
- 10 completed calls.
- 3 paid pilot asks.
- 1-2 paid pilots closed or clearly lost with reasons.
- 1 delivery proof packet reviewed before public use.

Pass means at least one buyer offers money, time, or a strong referral for a
concrete offer. Fail means people like the story but take no concrete next step.

## First 30 Days

Minimum target:

- 40 conversations.
- 10 written proposals.
- 3 paid pilots.
- $5k+ gross revenue.
- 1 public-safe case study.

Strong target:

- 80 conversations.
- 20 written proposals.
- 8 paid pilots.
- $40k+ gross revenue.
- 4 public-safe case studies.

If the foundry is below $5k after 30 days, narrow the offer and sell
founder-led services before building more platform.

## First 120 Days

Sales: three tested scripts, a simple CRM ledger, weekly pipeline review.

Delivery: repeatable pilot workflow, QA checklist, redaction checklist.

Product: operator workspace setup, essential-needs packet builder,
source-backed review kit.

Operators: 20-person role map, contribution ledger, training ladder.

Proof: five public-safe case studies or demos.

Resource pool: consent UX prototype only; no default pooling.
