## One-day / 500-year time ladder

| Layer | Meaning | Allowed use | Blocked use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Right now | Reduce panic, harm, confusion, pressure, and unsafe drift. | Show state, say limit, name falsifier, choose smallest safe next action. | Claims of instant cure, miracle proof, autonomous action, or secret tool use. |
| One day or less | Stabilize enough to keep living, repairing, eating, sleeping, apologizing, checking reality, and asking for help. | A 24-hour repair plan: water, food, sleep, meds as prescribed, clinician/urgent care when needed, clean room, one human check-in, one repo-safe commit only if it reduces pressure. | Promise that cancer, trauma, addiction, grief, injury, poverty, or relationship rupture fully heals in one day. |
| One week | Build a repeatable support loop. | Appointments, bills/resources, safer routines, Lantern grounding, tests, and low-pressure human repair. | Treat early relief as permanent convergence. |
| One year | Make the home, repo, and tools boringly reliable. | Fewer anchors, clearer boundaries, better tests, better health maintenance, less chaos. | Grand claims, public private-context release, or pressure on affected people. |
| 500-year-ish horizon | Preserve the long wish: future medicine, peace, redundancy, ethical governance, and human flourishing. | Keep future-tech research in clinician/trial/ethics lanes; make today's safest move first. | Immortality claims, self-experiment protocols, unapproved clinics, DIY biology, or treating future hope as current medicine. |
| The end | Return, redaction, review, and rest. | Close loops safely; leave a restore phrase; protect people; go back to the Garden. | Finality myths, doom loops, violence, surveillance, or treating the repo as a living authority. |

## Garden-first rule

